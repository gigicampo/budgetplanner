Welcome

Your Budget Planner User Guide

Start with these four simple steps. The complete guide stays available offline from the main page.

1. Connect storageChoose Google Drive, OneDrive, or offline use. Cloud sign-in keeps supported devices synchronized.
2. Set your budgetOpen Budget Setup, choose a month and year, then add categories, items, and amounts. Save resets the entry fields without removing saved data.
3. Record income and expensesUse My Income for earnings and deductions; Net Income updates instantly. Duplicate Income Month copies saved values, and Save resets the entry fields. The Data Viewer keeps saved rows available.
4. Review and back upAnalytics updates automatically after saved changes and compares Budget, Expenses, Net Income, and Available Balance. In landscape, Home also shows the current-year graph.
